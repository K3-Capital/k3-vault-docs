# Why K3 Vaults

K3 Capital has been deploying liquidity on-chain since 2021. The team originated as the internal decentralized-finance department of a large retail crypto lending platform and became a standalone company in 2024. That background combines institutional credit and risk discipline with hands-on execution in on-chain markets.

Vault infrastructure turns an actively managed strategy into a product that a distribution partner can offer through a consistent customer journey. It collects deposits and withdrawal requests, records ownership, and uses a common valuation point to determine what each request receives. K3 manages the portfolio under the mandate agreed for that specific vault and provides continuous automated monitoring with on-call response.

## Why internalize the infrastructure

Many managed products split strategy management, vault infrastructure, distribution, and customer experience across several providers. K3's direction is to bring strategy management and more of the delivery infrastructure together. This is intended to give partners:

- one route into K3-managed strategies;
- a reusable deposit, valuation, reporting, and withdrawal journey;
- fewer hand-offs between the strategy manager and the product infrastructure; and
- a foundation that can support additional eligible assets and EVM networks over time.

This is a long-term direction, not a statement that every K3 strategy already uses K3's proprietary vault technology or that every asset and network is supported.

## Today, next, and long term

### Today

The K3 cbBTC Vault is the first live deployment of K3's proprietary vault infrastructure. It runs on Ethereum and accepts cbBTC. K3 also manages live products and allocations delivered through partner infrastructure and external strategy venues.

### Next

K3 intends to package more vault-specific strategies behind a consistent interface for distribution partners. Each vault will define its own eligible customers, assets, risk parameters, operating policy, fees, and withdrawal process.

### Long term

The aim is a one-stop strategy and infrastructure layer for partners across additional eligible assets and EVM networks. Expansion depends on technical compatibility, product design, risk review, and the governing terms of each vault.

## Commercial model

Default commercial settings are **0% management fee and 5% performance fee**. Vault-specific terms prevail. The smart contract does not add a separate protocol-level fee; this does not mean a vault has no fees or costs.

K3 does not claim that this model always produces lower total fees than other infrastructure arrangements. Any comparison depends on the complete terms of the specific products being compared.
